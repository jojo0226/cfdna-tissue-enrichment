    Method

    Every panel runs exactly these steps; only the atlas layers and the pre-registered tracks differ.

    1. Coverage

    1. Grid. hg38 autosomes in 50 kb (45,595 bins) or 10 kb (241,026 bins). Bins are removed if they overlap the ENCODE blacklist (ENCFF356LFX) by any amount, overlap an assembly gap, are > 10% N, or have 45-mer mappability < 0.80.
    2. Counting. Fragment midpoints per bin; MAPQ ≥ 30 and length 50–500 bp. Midpoints in removed bins are dropped.
    3. Normalisation (identical to cfdna-enrich prep.py): log2(CPM + 1) → LOWESS on bin GC (frac 0.10, 3 iterations, level kept) → subtraction of a centred ~2 Mb rolling median per chromosome (removes copy number and other broad trends).

    2. Track specificity

    For each layer separately (one source, one assay), every bin's openness is standardised across that layer's tracks, and each track's specificity is its value minus the mean of the layer's other tracks. Specificity says how much more open a bin is in that track than in the other tracks of the same layer. Layers are never mixed in this step, so DNase and ATAC values are not compared directly.

    Panels can add derived tracks:

    • groups: mean specificity of member tracks (e.g. Wgroup_trophoblast_SCT_VCT, Tgroup_glioma);
    • axes: mean of one set of tracks minus another (e.g. origin_fetal_specific); a cross-layer axis (e.g. glioma_vs_blood, TCGA tracks minus ENCODE blood tracks) is allowed but marked exploratory.

    Within-layer means relative to the layer

    T_GBM specificity is "open in GBM more than in the other TCGA cancer types", not "more than in blood". Use a cross-layer axis or the C_ blood tracks alongside it when the question is tumour vs blood.

    3. The two modes

    mode per-sample score method
    sample yes Each bin is turned into a z-score against the reference group (median, 1.4826 × MAD; reference samples left out of their own reference when ≥ 4). E = Spearman correlation of a sample's z over all bins with the track's specificity. Uses all bins, nothing selected.
    differential no Each bin is fitted with the group model (moderated t). Per track: (a) all bins: Spearman correlation of bin t with specificity; (b) top bins: mean specificity of the 2,000 bins most up and most down. Both are tested against label permutations (bins refitted and top bins re-selected each time). A z against GC × mappability matched random bins is reported for description only.

    Positive values mean the same in both modes: more coverage, in cases (or in that sample), at bins specific to the track.

    4. Group design and tests

    • --factor COLUMN:REFERENCE:CASE defines a two-level factor; repeat for factorial designs. The reference group is every factor at its reference level.
    • Effect tests (sample mode): OLS of each score on the factors + covariates; p from OLS and from permutations of one factor's labels within levels of the other factors (covariates stay with their samples).
    • Pairwise tests: every other group vs the reference group (difference, AUC, Mann–Whitney p, permutation p).
    • Multiple testing: BH across all tracks (q_perm) and separately across the panel's primary tracks (q_perm_primary).

    5. Why there is no sets mode

    Versions ≤ 0.1.2 had a third mode, sets: mean coverage over each track's top-1,000 specific bins minus random bins matched on GC and mappability (the original cfdna-chromatin-xai marker method). It was removed in 0.2.0. Across 11 benchmarks its track rankings agreed only moderately with the other modes (r 0.35–0.63, against 0.91–0.97 between sample and differential), tracks significant in two modes sometimes went in opposite directions with sets the odd one out, and it was the least sensitive mode in the PRAD mixtures (detection limit 20% at 10 M fragments, against 2% for sample). Top specific bins differ from matched random bins in more than GC and mappability (gene density, replication timing), so part of a sets score is not cell-type signal.

    6. Why permutation p-values for top bins

    Top differential bins are selected from noisy data, so they are not exchangeable with random bins even when GC and mappability are matched. On null data, matched-random p-values flagged 6–14% of tracks at p < 0.05, against ~6% for the permutation p-values. The permutation null repeats the selection on shuffled labels.
